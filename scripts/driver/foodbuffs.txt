# Food Buffs: modded food can be eaten when full, gives its buffs, and never raises saturation above its own value.
# Needs Farmer's Delight in dev-driver/run/mods. Run: gradlew :dev-driver:runClient -PdriverScript=scripts/driver/foodbuffs.txt
# Each eat logs "[FoodBuffs test] ate ...: food a -> b, saturation x -> y (food's own saturation s), effects: ..."
waittitle
world foodtest
waitworld
chat /gamemode survival
# 1) vanilla bread while full: vanilla rule, must be refused (do it before any Farmer's Delight Nourishment)
chat /foodbuffs setfood 20 5
chat /item replace entity @s weapon.mainhand with minecraft:bread
wait 5
chat /foodbuffs eat
wait 20
# 2) cheap modded food while full, saturation 5: may be eaten, saturation must STAY 5 (vanilla would give 5.6)
chat /foodbuffs setfood 20 5
chat /item replace entity @s weapon.mainhand with farmersdelight:tomato
wait 5
chat /foodbuffs eat
wait 20
# 3) beef stew (own saturation 19.2) while full, saturation 5: vanilla would give 20, the cap gives 19.2
chat /foodbuffs setfood 20 5
chat /item replace entity @s weapon.mainhand with farmersdelight:beef_stew
wait 5
chat /foodbuffs eat
wait 20
# 4) hungry (food 6, saturation 0) + tier-4 fish stew: normal eating, buffs applied
chat /effect clear @s
chat /foodbuffs setfood 6 0
chat /item replace entity @s weapon.mainhand with farmersdelight:fish_stew
wait 5
chat /foodbuffs eat
wait 20
# tooltip check: hold a tier-5 dish and open the inventory
chat /item replace entity @s weapon.mainhand with farmersdelight:pasta_with_meatballs
screen inventory
wait 3
move 248 246
wait 3
screenshot fb-tooltip
screen none
quit
